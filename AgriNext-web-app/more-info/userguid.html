🌱 About Agri🌾Next

Agri🌾Next is a smart farming advisory platform designed to help Indian farmers make better agricultural decisions using modern technology. It combines crop recommendation, weather forecasting, disease detection, market price prediction and farming guidance in one system.

Who can use this platform?

  • Small and medium farmers
  • Agriculture students
  • Rural entrepreneurs
  • Anyone interested in smart farming

🔐 Login & Account Guide

  • Email Login: Login using registered email and password
  • Mobile OTP Login: Secure login using OTP
  • Google Login: Quick access using Google account
  • Sign Up: New users can easily create an account

Your personal data is handled securely.

🌾 Crop Recommendation System

Suggests suitable crops based on soil conditions, weather and region.

  • Enter required soil details
  • Submit information
  • Get recommended crops

🦠 Plant Disease Identification

Upload an image of the affected plant leaf to identify possible diseases and get basic guidance.

🌦️ Weather Forecast

Provides real-time weather updates to help farmers plan irrigation, sowing and harvesting.

📈 Market Price Prediction

Helps farmers understand crop price trends and decide the best selling time.
